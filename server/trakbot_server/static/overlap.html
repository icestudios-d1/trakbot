Active vs background usage

Separates what people are using from what is just playing behind their work.

Active vs passive time by app / site

Active: in the foreground while the person is present   Passive: playing audio behind another app, or after they stepped away

How to read this

Normal activity tracking credits the whole session to whichever window is in front. A tab left open for music then looks like heavy YouTube use. TrakBot separates the two:

Active time is only when the app or site is in front of someone who is not idle. Passive time is when a site is playing audio while something else is in front (Photoshop, Illustrator…) or the person is away.

Productivity percentages elsewhere in TrakBot use active time only, so background music never counts against anyone.

Detected from audible browser tabs via the TrakBot browser extension (v0.2+). Desktop music apps like Spotify are visible only while in front.
